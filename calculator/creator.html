Scenario summary

Fees assume the 3% transfer fee applies to every trade, with your share collected continuously. The holding is valued at market cap × your share × (1 − exit cost). Selling it in practice would move the price more than this. Your dev buy at launch costs roughly your holding share × launch market cap. None of this is financial advice.

How it's calculated

Weights. Each miner's weight is (mining $/day)exponent × level weight. Other miners are approximated as all earning the average, so a mix of very different GPUs would shift the result slightly. What's left out. $HASH price changes after payout, slippage on conversions, and the platform's PPS fee. GPU revenue figures for the 3060 Laptop, 4060, 4070 and 5090 come from project notes. The 4090 figure and all power draws are estimates, so edit them for your card. This is not financial advice, and nothing here is a promise of returns.

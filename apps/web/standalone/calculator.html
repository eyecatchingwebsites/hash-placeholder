Values are in dollars of $HASH at payout prices, before pool fees and slippage. About 75% of mining is paid every ~10 minutes; the rest after the mined coin confirms and sells, trued up to the real price. The chest is 2.5% of all $HASH volume, including the platform's own buys for payouts. Only the rules are fixed (1 : 2 : 4 weights, square-root balancing, a 5% cap per wallet); the dollar amounts are not.

Risk. $HASH is a highly volatile token and may lose most or all of its value. Payouts depend on trading volume, the number of miners, coin prices and pool results, and can fall to near zero. Mining uses electricity and adds heat and wear to your hardware. Example figures on this site are illustrations, not forecasts or promises. Nothing here is financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Check the rules where you live before taking part.

Security. We will never ask for your seed phrase or private key. The contract address is published only on this site.

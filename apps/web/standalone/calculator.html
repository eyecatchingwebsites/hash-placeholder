Mining   Cut of the tax

Values are in dollars of $HASH at payout prices, before pool fees and slippage. About 75% of mining is paid every ~10 minutes; the rest after the mined coin confirms and sells, trued up to the real price. The 5% tax splits into 0.5% development, a miner chest that targets 5× miners' mining (up to 3.5%), and holder rewards (the rest, at least 1%). Holder rewards assume your hold clock already meets your level. Only the rules are fixed (1 : 2 : 4 weights, square-root balancing for miners, a 5% cap per wallet); the dollar amounts are not.

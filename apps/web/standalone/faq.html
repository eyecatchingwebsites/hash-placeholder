Getting started

What is Hashcoin?

A Solana token ($HASH) and a Windows mining app that work together. A 5% tax on every $HASH trade pays two groups: people mining with their GPUs (the platform converts what they mine into $HASH and adds a cut of the tax) and people holding $HASH. It's built as a working product, not a joke coin.

What do I need?

Any Windows PC with an NVIDIA, AMD or Intel Arc graphics card: a gaming PC, a gaming laptop, an AI rig, or an editing or render workstation. Plus a Solana wallet such as Phantom or Solflare. You give the app your public wallet address and nothing else.

How do I start?

Install the app, paste your public Solana address and press Start. The app detects your GPUs, the server picks a coin for each card, and the app downloads a hash-verified miner and runs it. The app isn't released yet; join the waitlist on the home page to hear when it is.

Do I need to buy $HASH?

No. M1 only requires mining. Holding $50 or more makes you H1, which earns holder rewards and unlocks M2; $500 for 24 hours makes you H2 and unlocks M3.

When does it launch?

When the app and payouts are proven end to end: a devnet test of the tax, the split and payouts, a private test on real GPUs, and an unedited install-to-first-payout video. The date will appear in the countdown on the home page. No $HASH token exists yet.

Mining and coins

Which coin does my GPU mine?

Whichever pays your card the most. The server scores each coin by the revenue you can actually sell, after slippage and confirmation delays, and assigns a coin per card. Today newer NVIDIA cards mostly favor PRL, older NVIDIA cards QUAN, and most AMD and Intel cards QTC. The final list depends on miner and pool checks before launch.

How often does it switch coins?

Rarely. It re-checks every few hours and switches only when another coin pays clearly more (about 10–15%) for a sustained period. Assignments are signed, so a hijacked connection can't redirect your mining.

Where do the revenue numbers come from?

The calculator uses hashrate.no's 24-hour revenue for each card on its best coin, fetched October 3, 2026. These change daily with coin prices and network difficulty, and they're before electricity.

Will mining slow down my PC?

No. The miner only uses GPU power you aren't using. When a game, a render or an AI job needs the GPU, mining steps back and only your hashrate drops. Your apps keep running at full speed.

Does it work on laptops?

Yes. The calculator lists 45 laptop GPUs. hashrate.no measures only three of them (RTX 3060, 3070 and 3070 Ti Laptop); the others are estimated from the desktop card with the same chip and marked "est.". Laptops run hotter, so keep yours on a hard surface with the vents clear.

Does mining hurt my GPU?

Mining keeps your GPU busy when you aren't using it. That adds heat, power use and some wear, similar to long gaming sessions. Electricity is your cost. Temperature and power limits are planned for the app before release.

Payouts

How often am I paid?

About every 10 minutes, in $HASH, straight to your wallet. The exact cadence may change to hourly after launch week if transaction costs require it.

How is my mining credited?

By shares: the proof of work your GPU submits, which the pool verifies instantly and which can't be faked. You never send us crypto.

Why do I see "paid" and "pending"?

Mined coins take time to confirm and sell (10+ hours on some chains). So about 75% of your estimated mining value is paid right away from a float, and the remaining ~25% follows once the mined coin is sold, trued up to the actual sale price. If we overpaid, the platform absorbs it; we never claw back.

Why am I paid in $HASH and not the coin I mined?

Converting payouts to $HASH is what connects mining to the token. Each payout starts as a market buy of $HASH, and holding it is how you move up levels. What you do with it after that is up to you.

Can I check payouts myself?

Yes. Every payout is a Solana transaction. After launch the site lists them live with Solscan links, along with what each level was paid in the last 24 hours.

Levels and payouts

There are two ladders. Holder levels pay you a share of the holder pot for your bag. Miner levels multiply your share of the miner chest. You can climb either one, or both.

Holder pot share = bag × holder weight. Miner chest share = √(your GPU's USD earnings) × miner weight; the square root means a small card gets a bigger boost relative to what it mines. No wallet can take more than 5% of one payout; any excess goes to everyone else.

How is the 5% split between miners and holders?

0.5% always goes to development. Miners get exactly what it takes to pay them, on average, 5× what their GPUs mine, up to 3.5% of volume. Holders get everything else, and never less than 1%. When volume is high, miners hit 5× easily and holders get more; when it's low, miners get the full 3.5% and holders their 1%.

What is the hold clock?

It starts when $HASH first lands in your wallet and keeps running, even through price dips. Selling shrinks it in proportion: sell 25% of your bag and the clock drops by 25%; sell everything and it restarts from zero. Buying never moves it. Any transfer out counts as selling, including sending to another wallet, a liquidity pool or an exchange.

Does mined $HASH count toward the thresholds?

Yes. Your bag includes mined $HASH, valued at a ~1-hour average price and checked at each payout.

What happens if the price dips?

If your bag falls under a threshold, you drop to the level below. When it's back above, the level returns immediately, because your hold clock kept running.

How often are holders paid?

Miners are paid about every 10 minutes. Holder rewards go out hourly or daily (to be decided), because paying every holder every 10 minutes would cost too much in transaction fees.

Can I split my bag across wallets to earn more?

No. Holder rewards follow bag size directly, so ten small wallets earn the same as one big one, and moving tokens between wallets counts as selling, which shrinks the clock.

Why can't the tax be avoided?

Because the token program itself charges it on every transfer, in every pool, aggregator and wallet. Earlier tax-sharing coins failed when traders simply bought on other exchanges to dodge the tax. A Token-2022 transfer tax closes that gap.

Do your own payouts pay the tax?

Yes. We send a little extra so you receive the full amount, and the tax we pay flows back to the treasury.

Which launchpad or exchange will it be on?

Most likely our own Token-2022 token with a Raydium or Orca pool, since launchpads with built-in reward taxes don't seem to support 5%. Either way, any Solana wallet and aggregator such as Jupiter can trade it, and the tax applies everywhere.

How do I know the contract address is real?

It appears here first, at launch, and the header's "CA" button copies it. Any address posted before that, or anywhere that doesn't match this site, is not ours.

Safety and antivirus

Will you ever ask for my seed phrase?

Never. Not the app, not the website, not support. We also never DM first. Anyone asking for your seed phrase or private key is a scammer.

Why might my antivirus flag the app?

Many antivirus products label any crypto miner "potentially unwanted", even legitimate ones. The app is code-signed and open source, with published SHA-256 checksums and a VirusTotal link, and we submit each release to Microsoft and antivirus vendors. We never ask you to turn off your antivirus; at most you allow the Hashcoin folder.

What does the app do on my PC?

It detects your GPUs, asks the server which coin each should mine, downloads a miner whose hash matches a signed list, and runs it only after you press Start. It shows a tray icon, stops when you say, and uninstalls cleanly. Auto-start is off unless you turn it on. No packing, no obfuscation.

Where is the money held?

The treasury and tax authorities are in a multisig. The wallet that sends payouts holds only a small float. Every wallet we control will be listed on the home page at launch.

Risks

Please read this before taking part.

  • $HASH is volatile. Like any new token, it can lose most or all of its value.
  • Payouts vary. They depend on coin prices, pool results, how much $HASH is traded and how many people mine. They can fall to near zero.
  • Costs are yours. Electricity, heat and hardware wear. The 5% tax applies when you buy, sell or move $HASH.
  • Selling costs you level time. Selling shrinks your hold clock in proportion, so taking profit can drop you a holder level, and with it a miner level.
  • New software. Bugs are possible. The code is open so anyone can check it.
  • Rules where you live. Mining and tokens are regulated differently by country. Check yours.

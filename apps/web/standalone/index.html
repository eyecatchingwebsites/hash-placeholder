      Show the math behind the estimate

      Earnings depend on one ratio: daily volume ÷ GPUs mining. The estimate uses $750 per GPU.

      1. 4.5% of volume V, plus payout buys (a per GPU), goes to miners and holders.

        pot = 4.5% × (V + N × a)

      2. Miners get what lifts them to 5× their mining, capped at 3.5%.

        chest ÷ N = min(4 × a, 3.5% × (V ÷ N + a))

      3. Your cut: √(your GPU's $/day) × your level, against the average.

        your cut = chest ÷ N × √(your GPU) × level ÷ (√a × L)

      4. Holders get the rest. Only V ÷ N matters.

        holders ÷ N = 4.5% × (V ÷ N + a) − chest ÷ N

      V daily volume · N GPUs mining · a average GPU's $/day · L average miner level

      Why $750 per GPU
      V ÷ N = 30% × $1,000 ÷ 40% = $750

      30% a day, a new Solana token's first weeks. Usually lower later.
      Mcap / holder
      $1,000. A $1M token has about 1,000 holders.
      Holders mining
      40% of holders run the app.
      Average GPU
      a = $2.50/day, about an RTX 3080.
      Levels
      L = 2.1×: 30% M1, 50% M2, 20% M3.

      FAQ

      More in the full FAQ.

      Will mining slow down my PC?

      No. The miner only uses GPU power you aren't using. When a game or render needs the GPU, mining steps back and only your hashrate drops.

      Does it work on laptops and workstations?

      Yes. Any PC with an NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics card: gaming PCs, gaming laptops, AI rigs, editing and render machines.

      Does the $HASH I mine earn holder rewards?

      Yes. Mined $HASH lands in your wallet and counts as your bag, so you get the miner bonus and holder rewards at the same time.

      How is the tax split between miners and holders?

      It adjusts itself every payout. Miners get what tops them up to 5× what their GPUs mine (up to 3.5%). Holders get the rest, never less than 1%.

      Can I just hold without mining?

      Yes. Hold $50+ and you get a share of the holder rewards. Mining adds a second payout.

      How much will I make?

      It depends on trading volume and how many GPUs mine. The estimate uses launch-week trading. After launch, the site shows what each level was actually paid.

      What happens if I sell?

      You pay the 5% tax, and your hold clock shrinks by the share you sold.

      Is this a rug?

      The tax can never be raised. The dev bag is 1%, bought in public, locked 90 days and gets no payouts. Every payout is on-chain.

      FAQ

      More in the full FAQ.

      Can I just trade it without mining?

      Yes. It trades like any Solana token. But the 2.5% only goes to wallets that are mining, so holding without mining earns nothing from the chest.

      How much will I actually make?

      It depends on two things: how much $HASH is traded, and how many GPUs are mining. High volume with few miners means a big cut; low volume with lots of miners means a small one. The calculator shows the scenarios. Nobody can promise a number, and after launch the site will show what each level was actually paid in the last 24 hours.

      Is this a rug?

      The tax is enforced by the token program and can never be raised. The dev bag is 1%, bought in public and locked for 90 days, and it gets no chest payouts. The treasury is a multisig, the code is open source, and every payout is on-chain. Check it all on Solscan at launch.

      What happens if I sell?

      You pay the 3% tax like everyone else, and that wallet can never be Level 3 again. You can still mine at Level 1 or 2.

      Will my antivirus flag the app?

      Some antivirus products flag every crypto miner as "potentially unwanted". The app is code-signed and open source with published checksums. You never need to turn off your antivirus.

      When is launch and what's the CA?

      Launch is announced once the app and payouts are proven. The CA is posted on this site first. Any address posted before that isn't ours.

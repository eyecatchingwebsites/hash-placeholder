Most coins need buyers. $HASH makes its own.

GPUs mine whatever pays most, then buy $HASH on Solana, around the clock. Every trade pays a 2–5% tax that goes back to miners and holders.

  • Hold it. Holders get at least 1% of every trade. No GPU needed.
  • Mine it. Paid in $HASH, plus a bonus on top. No buying needed.
  • Or both. A bigger bag unlocks the bigger miner bonuses.

      Show the math behind the estimate

      Earnings depend on one ratio: daily volume ÷ GPUs mining. The estimate uses $750 per GPU.

      1. The tax rate r is the lowest of 2–5% that lifts miners to 5× and leaves holders 1%+ of volume.

        tax = r × (V + N × a)

      2. Dev gets 5–10% of it (less when the tax is higher). Miners get what lifts their average to 5×, kept within 45–75%.

        chest ÷ N = 4 × a, within 45–75% of tax ÷ N

      3. Your cut: √(your GPU's $/day) × your level, against the average.

        your cut = chest ÷ N × √(your GPU) × level ÷ (√a × L)

      4. Holders get the rest. Only V ÷ N matters.

        holders ÷ N = r × (V ÷ N + a) − dev ÷ N − chest ÷ N

      V daily volume · N GPUs mining · a average GPU's $/day · L average miner level

      Why $750 per GPU
      V ÷ N = 30% × $1,000 ÷ 40% = $750

      FAQ

      More in the full FAQ.

      Do I need a GPU?

      No. Hold $50+ and you share the holder rewards from every trade. Miners' share is capped, so busier trading means more for holders.

      Do I need to buy $HASH to mine?

      No. Mining pays you in $HASH plus a miner bonus. Holding a bag on top unlocks the 2× and 4× miner bonus and earns holder rewards.

      Why buy if I can just mine it?

      Mining alone stays at Level 1. The 2× and 4× miner bonus need a bag, and the bag itself earns holder rewards. Mining fills your bag slowly; buying gets you to the higher levels sooner.

      Does the $HASH I mine earn holder rewards?

      Yes. Mined $HASH lands in your wallet and counts as your bag, so you get the miner bonus and holder rewards at the same time.

      How is the tax split between miners and holders?

      It adjusts itself every payout. Miners get what lifts their average pay to 5× what their GPUs mine (45–75% of the tax). Holders get the rest, never less than 1% of every trade.

      What happens if I sell?

      You pay the tax (2–5%), and your hold clock resets.

      How much will I make?

      It depends on trading volume and how many GPUs mine. The estimate uses launch-week trading. After launch, the site shows what each level was actually paid.

      Will mining slow down my PC?

      No. The miner only uses GPU power you aren't using. When a game or render needs the GPU, mining steps back and only your hashrate drops.

      Does it work on laptops and workstations?

      Yes. Any PC with an NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics card: gaming PCs, gaming laptops, AI rigs, editing and render machines.

      Is this a rug?

      The tax can never be raised. The dev bag is 1%, bought in public, locked 90 days and gets no payouts. Every payout is on-chain.

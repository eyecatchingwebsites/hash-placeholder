      The estimate is one number: volume per GPU.

      What miners and holders earn depends on how much $HASH trades and how many GPUs are mining. Work through the formula and those two only appear as one ratio: daily volume ÷ GPUs mining.

      1. 4.5% of the day's volume V, plus the platform's own payout buys (about a per GPU), goes to miners and holders.

        pot = 4.5% × (V + N × a)

      2. Miners get what lifts them to 5× their mining (4 × a per GPU on top), but never more than 3.5%.

        chest ÷ N = min(4 × a, 3.5% × (V ÷ N + a))

      3. Your share of the chest: √(your GPU's $/day) × your miner level, against the average.

        your cut = chest ÷ N × √(your GPU) × level ÷ (√a × L)

      4. Holders get everything else, split by bag × holder level. N cancels out everywhere except in V ÷ N:

        holders ÷ N = 4.5% × (V ÷ N + a) − chest ÷ N

      V = daily volume · N = GPUs mining · a = average mining GPU's earnings per day · L = average miner level

      Why $750 per GPU
      V ÷ N = (V ÷ mcap) × (mcap ÷ holders) ÷ (miners ÷ holders) = 30% × $1,000 ÷ 40% = $750

      Market cap cancels out. The same estimate holds for a $500K coin or a $50M coin, as long as people trade and mine in the same proportions.

      Plugged in

      Built so the tax actually pays out.

      The tax can't be dodged.

      Most tax tokens only charge in their own pool, so traders route around them and the rewards dry up. $HASH uses Solana's Token-2022 transfer fee, charged on every transfer, everywhere.

      It balances itself.

      Miners are paid up to 5× their mining. Anything beyond that goes to holders, and holders always get at least 1%. No one decides the split by hand; it's a public formula.

      Miners are buyers.

      Every payout starts as a market buy of $HASH, and miners need $HASH in their wallet to reach M2 and M3. More GPUs mining means more buying.

      FAQ

      More in the full FAQ.

      Can I just hold it without mining?

      Yes. Hold $50 or more and you earn holder rewards: at least 1% of every trade is split between holders by bag size and level (H1, H2, H3). Mining adds a second payout on top.

      How much will I actually make?

      It depends on one ratio: daily volume divided by GPUs mining. The estimate uses $750 of volume per GPU, which is launch-week trading; the math is here. Miners are paid up to 5× their mining, and holders get the rest. When volume is lower, holders get less first, down to their 1% minimum, then miners. After launch the site shows what each level was actually paid in the last 24 hours instead.

      Is this a rug?

      The tax is enforced by the token program and can never be raised. The dev bag is 1%, bought in public and locked for 90 days, and it gets no miner or holder payouts. The treasury is a multisig, the code is open source, and every payout is on-chain. Check it all on Solscan at launch.

      What happens if I sell?

      You pay the 5% tax like everyone else, and your hold clock shrinks by the share you sold. Sell 25% of your bag and the clock drops 25%; sell everything and it restarts. Your levels drop if you fall under a threshold.

      Will my antivirus flag the app?

      Some antivirus products flag every crypto miner as "potentially unwanted". The app is code-signed and open source with published checksums. You never need to turn off your antivirus.

      When is launch and what's the CA?

      Launch is announced once the app and payouts are proven. The CA is posted on this site first. Any address posted before that isn't ours.

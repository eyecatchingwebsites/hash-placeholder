How it works

Your GPU earns. Traders' fees add to it.

Install and paste your wallet

Download the signed Windows app and paste your Solana address. Only the public address is needed. Hashcoin never asks for your seed phrase.

Your card mines the best coin

Our server checks every few minutes which coin pays your exact card best (Pearl, Quantum and others) and switches only when it's clearly worth it.

We convert it to $HASH

What your GPU mines is sold and used to buy $HASH on the open market. Every miner adds steady buy pressure.

Paid every 10 minutes

Mining earnings land in your wallet every 10 minutes, plus your share of the miner chest, which grows with your level.

The loop

Mining feeds the token. The token feeds the miners.

Every $HASH trade pays a 3% fee that's built into the token itself, so it applies on every exchange and can't be routed around. 2.5% goes into the miner chest and is paid out in $HASH. At the same time, everything miners mine is used to buy $HASH.

Who it's for

Two ways in.

Traders

What's behind $HASH

  • Constant buying. Every connected GPU's mining revenue is used to buy $HASH, around the clock.
  • Holders have a reason to stay. Miners who hold reach higher levels and bigger payouts. Selling locks a wallet out of Level 3 for good.
  • A fee nobody can dodge. The 3% fee is enforced by the Solana token program on every transfer, on every exchange.
  • Everything on-chain. Payouts, the chest and the dev wallet are public. Check them on Solscan.
Miners

What your gaming PC gets

  • One install. No pools, wallets or command lines to configure. Paste your address and press Start.
  • Best coin, per card. NVIDIA, AMD and Intel Arc cards each mine what pays them most, and it switches automatically.
  • Paid every 10 minutes in $HASH, plus a share of the miner chest.
  • Smaller cards get a boost. Chest shares use square-root balancing, so a 3060 earns a bigger multiple than a 5090.
Levels

Hold more, for longer, earn a bigger share.

Your share of the miner chest depends on your GPU and your level. Mined $HASH counts toward your level, so holding what you earn moves you up.

Level 1 1× Just mine

Everyone starts here. You get your mining earnings plus a base share of the chest.

Level 2 2× Hold $50 of $HASH

Double chest weight. Most miners get here within days just by holding their payouts.

Level 3 4× Hold $500 · 14 days · never sold

Four times the chest weight, for wallets that have held for 14 days without ever selling or transferring out.

Dips don't reset you. If your position falls under $500 you drop to Level 2, and you're back at Level 3 the moment it's above $500 again.

Selling is final for Level 3. Any sale or transfer out of the wallet removes it from Level 3 permanently. Level 1 and 2 still apply.

Fair caps. No single wallet can take more than 5% of the chest, so big farms can't crowd everyone out.

FAQ

Questions.

What do I need?

A Windows PC with an NVIDIA RTX/GTX, AMD Radeon or Intel Arc graphics card and current drivers, plus a Solana wallet address. Laptops work too, but earn less and run warmer.

Why does my antivirus warn about it?

Some antivirus products flag every crypto miner as a "potentially unwanted application", because malware sometimes mines in secret. Hashcoin is signed, open source, only mines when you press Start, and shows a tray icon while running. If your antivirus blocks it, you can allow the Hashcoin folder only. Never turn your antivirus off.

How much will I earn?

It depends on your card, which coins pay best that day, how much $HASH is traded, how many people mine, and your level. The example above shows the range. Earnings are paid in $HASH, whose price moves, so we never promise an amount.

Why am I paid in $HASH and not the coin I mined?

Converting mined coins to $HASH is what creates steady buying for the token, and the miner chest is paid in $HASH too. You can hold it to level up, or sell it, keeping in mind that any sale ends Level 3 for that wallet.

Does mining hurt my GPU?

The app keeps temperature and power limits on by default, similar to normal gaming load. Fans will run. You can pause anytime, or let it pause automatically while you play.

What are the risks?

$HASH is a memecoin and can lose most or all of its value. Payouts fall when trading volume falls or more people join. Electricity costs money. Only take part with what you're comfortable losing, and check your local rules on crypto and taxes.

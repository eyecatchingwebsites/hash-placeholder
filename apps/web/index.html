A new way to mine at home · Pre-launch

Home mining, multiplied.

Your GPU mines. Hashcoin turns it into $HASH and pays you every ~10 minutes. On top of that, 2.5% of every $HASH trade goes into a chest that only miners share.

How it works

One loop. Each part feeds the next.

Miners create buying. Buying and selling create fees. Fees fill the chest. The chest pays miners. No step depends on a promise; each one is a transaction you can check on-chain.

  1. 01

    Your GPU mines

    The server picks the best-paying coin for each card (today that's PRL, QUAN or QTC, depending on the card) and switches only when another coin pays clearly more for a sustained period.

  2. 02

    Converted to $HASH

    The platform sells what you mine and uses the proceeds to buy $HASH on the open market. You're credited by shares, the proof of work the pool verifies instantly.

  3. 03

    Every trade pays 3%

    $HASH is a Solana Token-2022 token with a transfer fee. The token program charges it on every transfer, in every pool, aggregator and wallet, so nobody can trade around it.

  4. 04

    2.5% fills the chest

    2.5% goes to the miner chest and 0.5% funds development. No burn, no hidden split. The chest is shared each epoch by the square root of GPU earnings times your level.

  5. 05

    You're paid

    About 75% of your estimated mining value arrives every ~10 minutes, plus your chest share. The rest follows once the mined coin confirms and sells, trued up to the real price.

Three steps to start

Install the app

One signed Windows installer, about 10 MB. It detects your GPUs and downloads a hash-verified miner for each one.

Paste your wallet address

Your public Solana address, from Phantom, Solflare or any wallet. Never a seed phrase or private key.

Press Start

Mining starts only when you press Start. Payouts land in your wallet about every 10 minutes. Stop whenever you like.

The miner app

Paste your wallet. Press Start.

A small Windows app that does the hard parts: picks the coin, runs the miner, and shows what's paid and what's pending.

  • Signed and open source. Code-signed installer, published SHA-256 checksums and a VirusTotal link for every release.
  • Hash-verified miners. Each miner download is checked against a signed list before it runs.
  • You're in control. Mines only after you press Start. Tray icon, Stop any time, clean uninstall. Auto-start is off by default.

Levels

Hold more, for longer, and your share of the chest grows.

Everyone who mines is Level 1. Holding $HASH moves you up. Each level multiplies your weight in the chest: 1×, 2× or 4×.

Level 1

1×
  • Mine with the app
  • No holding needed

Level 2

2×
  • Mine with the app
  • Hold at least $50 of $HASH (mined $HASH counts)

Level 3

4×
  • Mine with the app
  • Hold at least $500 of $HASH
  • 14 days since $HASH first landed in the wallet
  • Never sold or transferred $HASH out

Dips don't reset you. Below $500 you drop to Level 2. Back above $500, Level 3 returns immediately, with no new 14-day wait.

Selling ends Level 3 for that wallet. Any transfer out counts: another wallet, an LP or an exchange. Level 1 and 2 stay open. Buying never counts against you.

Fair to small GPUs. Weight uses the square root of GPU earnings, so a small card gets a bigger boost relative to what it mines.

No whales. One wallet can take at most 5% of a chest. Position value uses a ~1-hour average price.

FAQ

Questions.

The short answers. The full FAQ and docs cover payouts, levels, the token and safety in detail.

What do I need?

A Windows PC with an NVIDIA, AMD or Intel Arc GPU, and a Solana wallet such as Phantom or Solflare. You only ever give the app your public address.

How much will I earn?

It depends on your GPU, your level, how much $HASH is traded and how many people are mining. Mining revenue is real and changes daily. The chest share can be large when trading is busy and small when it's quiet. There is no guaranteed amount. After launch, the site shows what each level was actually paid in the last 24 hours.

Why am I paid in $HASH and not the coin I mined?

Converting every payout to $HASH is what ties mining to the token: each payout is a market buy, and holding $HASH is what moves you up levels. You can do whatever you like with it, but selling or transferring it out permanently ends Level 3 for that wallet.

Why might my antivirus warn about it?

Many antivirus products flag any crypto miner as "potentially unwanted", even legitimate ones. Our app is code-signed and open source, with published checksums, and we submit every release to Microsoft and antivirus vendors. We never ask you to disable your antivirus; at most you allow the Hashcoin folder.

Does mining hurt my GPU?

Mining runs your GPU at a steady load, which adds heat, power use and some wear, much like long gaming sessions. Electricity costs are yours. Temperature and power limits and pause-while-gaming are planned for the app before release.

What are the risks?

$HASH is a new, volatile token and can lose most or all of its value. Payouts can fall to near zero if trading slows. The 3% fee applies when you sell. Only take part with money and hardware you can afford to put at risk.

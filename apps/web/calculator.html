Values are in dollars of $HASH at payout prices, before pool fees and slippage. About 75% of mining is paid every ~10 minutes; the rest after the mined coin confirms and sells, trued up to the real price. The miner chest is 2.5% of all $HASH volume, including the platform's own buys for payouts. Only the rules are fixed (1 : 2 : 4 weights, square-root balancing, a 5% cap per wallet); the dollar amounts are not.
